About Us

At Minimalist Travels, we believe the journey should be as peaceful as the destination. Founded by Mithul Vaibhav, our agency was built to strip away the noise and clutter of modern tourism. We handle the complex logistics of global exploration—bringing absolute clarity to your itinerary—so you can focus entirely on a minimalist, stress-free experience.


Our Mission


To simplify global exploration by removing the anxiety of travel planning. We meticulously design every aspect of your journey, ensuring that from departure to return, your only responsibility is to enjoy the moment.


Our Vision


To become the world's most trusted partner for mindful travelers, proving that true luxury is found in simplicity, expert curation, and uninterrupted peace.


Our Story


Minimalist Travels began with a realization by our founder, Mithul Vaibhav: the modern travel industry has become overwhelming. Between endless booking tabs, hidden fees, and overcrowded itineraries, the actual joy of discovery was being lost. We started this agency to offer an antidote. By focusing on quality over quantity, we curate a select number of premium destinations, ensuring each experience is authentic, deeply restorative, and entirely seamless.


The Minimalist Approach


We do not believe in packing your day from dawn until dusk. Instead, our itineraries are designed with breathing room. We hand-select boutique accommodations, partner with local experts who respect the environment, and arrange private transfers so you never have to rush. We handle the visas, the tickets, and the reservations in the background.


Why Travel With Us?


We do not just book flights and hotels; we design seamless narratives. Whether you are seeking a quiet cabin retreat in the Swiss Alps or a curated beachfront escape in Bali, our dedicated team manages the complexities in the background. We value your time and peace of mind above all else. With Minimalist Travels, you just pack your bags—we take care of the rest.
